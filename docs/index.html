Save my Ora

Ora (ora.pm) shuts down on Saturday, October 31, 2026. Save your projects, their order, your custom fields, the history and comments of every task, Team Chat and your attachments. Two parts, in this order: the bookmark saves your data (steps 1 and 2), then a small program downloads your attached files (step 3).

  1. Drag this button to your bookmarks bar

    Save my Ora

    Bookmarks bar hidden? Show it with ⌘ Shift B (Windows and Linux: Ctrl Shift B). In Safari: View → Show Favorites Bar.

  2. Open Ora and click the bookmark

    Go to app.ora.pm, log in, then click Save my Ora in your bookmarks bar, and Start a new backup in the box that appears. A box shows the progress. Leave the tab open until it says Done.

    It takes a while: about 3 requests per task, so roughly 1 to 2 hours for 6,000 tasks (a few minutes for a small account). The box shows the time left. Meanwhile:

    • keep the Ora tab visible: a hidden tab runs much slower. To use other tabs, move the Ora tab to its own window and don't minimize it;
    • keep your computer awake and plugged in, lid open. On a Mac, you can also type caffeinate -d in Terminal and leave it open until the end;
    • if the connection drops, the bookmark reconnects by itself and continues. If you must stop, click Stop and save now: you get a ZIP with everything saved so far. To continue later, click the bookmark again, choose Resume from a ZIP… and pick that ZIP: the backup goes on exactly where it stopped and gives you a complete ZIP.

    The file ora-backup-<date>.zip lands in your Downloads folder. It holds, for each project, Ora's official JSON export and a spreadsheet (CSV), plus what those exports leave out. You don't need Ora's own export buttons anymore.

  3. Download the attachments

    Your attached files (images, PDFs…) are not in the ZIP: they are stored separately by Ora, and a web page is not allowed to download them. Get the small program that does it:

    Download ora_attachments.py

    It lands in your Downloads folder too. Then, on a Mac:

    Open Terminal (⌘ Space, type Terminal, Return), type python3 followed by a space, drag ora_attachments.py into the window, then press Return.

    It finds the ZIP by itself and puts the files in Documents → Ora - Attachments. Windows, Linux and help.

What is in the ZIP, and is it safe?

The ZIP holds the official Export as JSON file of every project you administer (the same file as Ora's own button), a spreadsheet (.csv) of every project with all its tasks, archived ones included, and ora-complement.json: what the exports leave out (the order of lists and tasks, custom fields, workflows and column automations, views, sprints, recurring tasks, templates, folders, favorites, Team Chat with archived channels, Git commits, your preferences), for every project you can see, and for every task its history, comments, timers and watchers. Nothing reads the complement yet: keep it, it lets a future import rebuild your projects as they were.

The bookmark only reads: every request is checked against a fixed list of read requests. It talks only to Ora, with the session you are logged in with, one request at a time. Nothing is sent anywhere else and your session key is not saved in the file, nor any other key or password (integration tokens are removed from your account data). Read the code.

The ZIP contains your teammates' names and email addresses and your chat messages: do not share it.
